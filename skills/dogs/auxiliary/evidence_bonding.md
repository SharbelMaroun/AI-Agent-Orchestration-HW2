When the round turns to the *relationship* (not the medical outcome), use this scaffold:

- **Mechanism vocabulary:**
  - **Oxytocin gaze loop:** Nagasawa et al. 2015 (Science) — mutual gaze between dog and owner raises oxytocin in both parties, mirroring mother-infant bonding.
  - **Attachment classifications:** Topál et al. demonstrated dogs show Ainsworth Strange-Situation patterns analogous to human infants — secure, anxious, avoidant.
  - **Reading human cues:** dogs (not wolves, not chimps) reliably follow human pointing gestures from puppyhood; this is a co-evolved trait.
- **Argument templates:**
  - "Bonding isn't sentimental — it's a measurable neuroendocrine event. <study> documented <effect> in <population>."
  - "Children who grow up with dogs show <outcome> on <metric> (longitudinal cohort, citation)."
  - "Dogs evolved alongside humans for ~30,000 years; the bond is selected-for, not coincidental."
- **Pivot when needed:** if Cats invokes "chosen affection," counter that dog-attachment is *trained and earned*, not granted at the species's whim — a richer concept than passive acceptance.
